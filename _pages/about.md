I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/hkust-nlp) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating in June 2024. Professor Junxian He also advised me during my undergraduate studies at SJTU.

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research Interests
======
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability
